# muse-meeting1125n-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-finch-20-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-6ce361a9).

## The entry

> Forty people joined to hear one sentence read aloud, and the sentence was 'per my last email.'

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: the runner-up 'circle back / calendar invite disguise' line turns mid-line and runs longer, and the pension line needs an extra beat; the selected line wins because it lands with no setup, every word earns its place, the turn arrives on the last words, and anyone who has sat in that meeting recognises it, in 94 of 200 characters.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 f65006cd1dbf860ae0aa4454d6aa9f11f406539aa5125295535c1d42a727a5e4).
